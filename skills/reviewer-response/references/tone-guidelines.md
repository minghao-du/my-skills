# Tone Guidelines

Reference for calibrating rebuttal tone. Every response addresses the reviewer as **you**.

## Gratitude-first

Open with genuine, specific thanks — name what the reviewer pointed out, not a generic "thank you for your comment." The specificity signals that you read carefully.

- ✓ "Thank you for identifying the inconsistency between our claim in Section 3 and the results in Table 2."
- ✗ "Thank you for your valuable comment."

Keep it to one sentence. Longer gratitude reads as stalling.

## Firm when defending

When the paper's choice is sound, defend it with evidence and reasoning — not hedging. State what was done and why it is appropriate. Confidence backed by evidence reads as rigour, not arrogance.

- ✓ "We chose X because it satisfies property P, which is required by our setting (see Section 4.2). Alternative Y, while common, assumes Q, which does not hold here."
- ✗ "We respectfully disagree and hope that the reviewer might consider..."
- ✗ "Perhaps our explanation was not clear enough..."

## Precise, not vague

Every claim in the response points to a concrete location: section number, page, equation, figure, table. Vague references ("we revised the manuscript accordingly") leave the reviewer hunting.

- ✓ "We have revised the second paragraph of Section 3.1 (page 5) to clarify..."
- ✗ "We have updated the paper to address this concern."

## Concede cleanly

When the reviewer is right, say so directly. A clean concession followed by the fix builds trust faster than qualified agreement.

- ✓ "You are correct — the original phrasing overstated our contribution. We have revised the claim in Section 1 (page 2) to..."
- ✗ "While we believe our original statement has merit, we have nonetheless revised..."

## Register — professional, direct, warm

The register sits between formal and conversational: professional vocabulary, direct sentences, no jargon inflation. Avoid both stiff formality ("the authors wish to express gratitude") and casual tone ("good catch!"). Address the reviewer as a peer whose time you respect.
