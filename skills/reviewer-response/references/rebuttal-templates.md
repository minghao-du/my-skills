# Rebuttal Templates

Example responses demonstrating the gratitude → substance → changes structure. Adapt phrasing to match the response letter's existing style.

---

## Accepting a suggestion

> **Comment:** The motivation in Section 2 is unclear. It would help to explain why existing approaches fail before introducing your method.

Thank you for pointing out that the motivation in Section 2 needed strengthening. You are right that readers benefit from understanding why existing approaches fall short before encountering our method.

We have added a paragraph at the beginning of Section 2 (page 3) that discusses the key limitations of prior work — specifically, their reliance on assumption A, which does not hold in setting S. This provides the necessary context for our approach.

**Changes:** Section 2, page 3, new paragraph added before Section 2.1.

## Defending a methodological choice

> **Comment:** Why did you not compare against method X? It seems like a natural baseline.

Thank you for raising this point. Method X operates under assumption A (as noted in [reference]), which is violated in our experimental setting because of property P. Including it as a baseline would require modifying its core mechanism, making the comparison misleading rather than informative.

We have added a discussion of this distinction in Section 5.1 (page 9) to clarify why method X is not directly comparable.

**Changes:** Section 5.1, page 9, new paragraph explaining the inapplicability of method X.

---

## Clarifying a misunderstanding

> **Comment:** The paper claims X outperforms Y, but Table 3 shows otherwise.

Thank you for flagging this. The apparent discrepancy arises because Table 3 reports results under setting S₁, while our claim refers to setting S₂ (Table 4, page 8). We recognise that this was not clearly stated.

We have revised the claim in Section 4 (page 6) to explicitly reference Table 4 and setting S₂, and added a footnote to Table 3 noting which setting it covers.

**Changes:** Section 4, page 6, revised claim with explicit table reference; Table 3, page 7, added footnote.

---

## Addressing a request for additional content

> **Comment:** It would strengthen the paper to include an analysis of failure cases.

Thank you for this suggestion. We agree that understanding failure modes adds value.

We have added Section 6.3 (page 12) presenting three representative failure cases, each with a brief analysis of the underlying cause. The cases were selected to cover the three error categories identified in our qualitative analysis.

**Changes:** Section 6.3, pages 12–13, new subsection with failure case analysis.
